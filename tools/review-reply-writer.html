Why replying matters.

Google notices

Replying to reviews is one of the signals that keeps your Business Profile active, which helps you show up in local searches and on Maps.

Customers read them

People read your replies to see how you treat customers, especially when something went wrong. A calm reply to a bad review builds a lot of trust.

It takes seconds

The hard part is knowing what to say. Let the tool draft it, tweak a word or two, and post.

Good questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Your first reply is free with no sign-up. Pop your email in once and you get three options for every review, as many times as you like.

Do you keep my reviews?

No. The review is sent to the AI to write a reply and isn't stored by us.

How should I handle a fake or abusive review?

Reply calmly and briefly, then flag it in your Google Business Profile as violating policy. Don't argue in public.

Can this happen automatically?

Yes. Tim's care plan clients get an AI brand assistant in their client portal that drafts review replies, captions and Google posts in their own voice.

Want more reviews to reply to?

Tim sets up Google Business Profiles, review links and QR codes for Perth businesses, so happy customers actually leave a review.
