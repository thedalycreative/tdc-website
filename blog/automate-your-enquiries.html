Never miss a lead: how to automate your enquiries.

The business that replies first often wins. Five simple automations that turn website enquiries into booked jobs.

Tim DalyAugust 20263 min read

A customer fills in your contact form. The email lands in a busy inbox. You see it that night, mean to reply in the morning, and then a job runs late. By the time you call, they've already booked someone else.

Speed matters with enquiries. The business that replies first often wins. The good news: you don't need to be glued to your phone. A little automation can do a lot of the work for you.

Step 1: reply instantly

Set up an automatic reply that goes out the moment someone submits your form. Make it warm and useful: thank them, tell them when you'll be in touch, and give them something helpful, like your price guide or a link to recent work. The customer knows they've been heard, and they're less likely to keep shopping around.

Step 2: send it to the right place

Instead of enquiries sitting in your email, send them automatically to wherever you manage jobs: a CRM, a spreadsheet, a job management app or a shared inbox. Everything ends up in one list, and nothing gets lost.

Step 3: get alerted for important ones

Not every enquiry is urgent. But a large commercial job, or someone who ticked "as soon as possible", might deserve a text message to your phone. Simple rules can flag them for you.

Step 4: follow up automatically

Many quotes are never accepted simply because nobody followed up. An automatic, friendly check-in a few days after sending a quote can win back jobs you'd otherwise lose. Make sure it stops as soon as the customer replies.

Step 5: ask for a review

When a job is marked complete, a short message asking for a Google review can go out automatically. More recent reviews help you get found, as I explained in my local SEO guide.

What you need

Often less than you'd think. Many businesses can do this with tools they already pay for, such as their website form, email, and a simple automation service that connects apps together. AI can help too, for example by summarising long enquiries or drafting a first reply for you to check.

Keep a human touch

Automation should make you faster and more reliable, not robotic. Keep messages short and friendly, write them in your own voice, and always make it easy to reach a real person.
